Welcome

DivinaECO Pack
in Greece

DivinaECO Pack brings 35 years of manufacturing expertise to Greece. We produce premium paper cups and sustainable food packaging solutions made from environmentally friendly materials, ideal for cafes, restaurants, takeaway outlets, coffee shops, retail chains, airlines, and FMCG brands.

We export to 3+ continents and proudly serve 100+ satisfied customers worldwide, delivering quality, reliability, and innovative packaging solutions.
