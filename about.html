About DivinaECO Pack

Who We Are

DivinaECO Pack IKE is a premium eco-friendly packaging company in Greece, backed by over three decades of continuous manufacturing heritage.

We operate at the intersection of environmental responsibility and product excellence. Every cup, lid and sleeve we produce carries a clear declaration: that commercial success and ecological care are not in competition — they belong together.

Serving cafes, restaurants, hotels, airlines, HORECA chains and FMCG brands, we bring the scale and consistency of a global manufacturer together with the responsiveness of a dedicated local partner — based in Kifisia, Athens, always within reach.

Our vertically integrated model means that from raw material procurement to quality-controlled dispatch, every stage happens under our direct supervision. No third parties. No compromises.

Our Journey

Three Decades of Growth

From a single production line to a global eco-packaging brand — a story of persistence, innovation and an unwavering belief that packaging can be better.

1992
1992

Founded

DivinaECO Pack is established — a bold start built on a single manufacturing unit and an unshakeable belief that quality and responsibility can coexist. This is where the 35-year journey begins: with one factory, one team, and one purpose.

1996
1996

Manufacturing Unit 1 — Plastic Products

Four years in, we commission our first dedicated manufacturing line for plastic products. This unit lays the operational blueprint that would be refined and replicated across every facility that followed — disciplined production, rigorous quality control, zero compromise on output standards.

2003
2003

Manufacturing Unit 2 — Packaged Drinking Water

Expansion into packaged drinking water marks our first significant diversification. Unit 2 demonstrates our capability to move beyond a single product category and build the infrastructure to serve different sectors — setting the precedent for the multi-unit model we operate today.

2007
2007

National Expansion — Across India

A defining moment: DivinaECO Pack scales its distribution and commercial reach across India. What began as a local operation becomes a nationally recognised brand, building the network relationships, logistics experience and market knowledge that would later power our international growth.

2016
2016

Paper Cup Unit 3 — The Eco Era Begins

The launch of Unit 3 and our first dedicated paper cup production line is a turning point in the company's identity. We pivot intentionally and permanently toward eco-friendly packaging — investing in plant-based PLA coatings, FSC-certified paperboard and the processes that would define DivinaECO Pack for the next decade.

2018
2018

Global Warehouse — US · UK · Europe · Africa

DivinaECO Pack crosses continents. With warehouse facilities established across the United States, United Kingdom, Europe and Africa, we become a genuinely global supplier — capable of fulfilling orders at scale with local delivery lead times across five continents. The foundation for serving Greece is now firmly laid.

2021
2021

Corrugation Unit 4 — Expanded Product Range

Unit 4 brings corrugated packaging fully in-house, dramatically expanding our product range. Grill boxes, food-service cartons, outer packaging for retail — all now manufacturable under one roof. This investment cements our position as a full-spectrum eco-packaging supplier rather than a single-product manufacturer.

2024
2024

Grill Boxes Unit 5 — Latest Manufacturing Unit

Our most recent manufacturing unit, Unit 5, is commissioned in 2024 with a dedicated focus on high-performance grill box and food packaging production. State-of-the-art machinery, tighter tolerances, faster output. This is DivinaECO Pack at full maturity — 32 years of accumulated manufacturing intelligence.

2026

Greece Expansion —
DivinaECO Pack IKE · Athens

The launch of DivinaECO Pack IKE in Greece is the next chapter of a 35-year story. Registered in Kifisia, Athens, and backed by five manufacturing units, a global logistics network and two dedicated partners on the ground, we bring internationally proven standards directly to the Greek HORECA sector, food service industry and retail market. Every client we serve here carries the weight of everything that came before.

"From a single factory in 1992 to five continents in 2026 — every step was taken with one purpose: to produce packaging that businesses and the planet can both be proud of."

Production Excellence

Everything
Under One Roof

Unlike distributors who resell third-party products, DivinaECO operates its own end-to-end production line. This vertical integration gives us complete authority over quality, lead times and the flexibility to deliver custom orders at a level pure distributors cannot match.

  • Single, Double & Triple Wall Cup Lines — hot beverages, cold drinks and specialty coffee, all formed in-house.
  • Offset & Flexo Printing up to 4 Colours — consistent, precise reproduction of your brand identity on every unit.
  • Plant-Based PLA Inner Lining — biodegradable, food-safe coating. No conventional plastics in our range.
  • Grill Boxes & Food Packaging — grease-resistant kraft solutions for HORECA and food service operations.
  • Batch Quality Control — dimensional accuracy, leak integrity and print fidelity verified before every dispatch.
