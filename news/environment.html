Sustainability

100% Recyclable Materials — Our Green Commitment

DivinaECO Pack · Environmental Policy

At DivinaECO Pack, sustainability is not a marketing promise — it is the foundation of every product we design, every material we source, and every partnership we build.

The packaging industry carries a significant environmental responsibility. Cups, lids and sleeves used daily in cafés, restaurants and food service operations add up to billions of units a year. We believe that every one of those units is an opportunity to do better — and we have built our entire business around that belief.

Our Material Philosophy

Every product in the DivinaECO range is manufactured from materials that are either fully recyclable, biodegradable, or compostable. We do not offer conventional single-use plastic-lined cups. Our paper cups use a plant-based PLA lining derived from renewable resources, which breaks down in industrial composting facilities — and in many cases, in home composting conditions as well.

Biodegradable — What It Actually Means

The term "biodegradable" is widely misused in the packaging industry. At DivinaECO, we apply a strict standard: a product is only described as biodegradable if it carries third-party certification confirming it breaks down within a defined timeframe, under specified conditions, leaving no harmful residue.

Our BIO Cups range, for example, carries both EN 13432 (industrial composting) and OK Compost Home certification, meaning they break down fully within 12 weeks in home composting conditions — verified independently, not self-declared.

We hold ourselves to a simple rule: if we wouldn't be comfortable explaining exactly how a product breaks down and where it ends up, we do not sell it. Every eco claim on our packaging is backed by certified third-party testing.
