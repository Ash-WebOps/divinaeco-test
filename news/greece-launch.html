DivinaECO Pack launches in Greece — A new chapter in eco-friendly packaging

01 March 2026 · DivinaECO Pack Team

We are delighted to announce the official launch of DivinaECO Pack in the Greek market, bringing 25 years of manufacturing expertise and a full range of certified eco-friendly packaging solutions.

After months of preparation and a careful study of the Greek and wider Mediterranean market, DivinaECO Pack is now officially operational in Greece. Our Athens-based office serves as the regional hub for Southern Europe, making it easier than ever for Greek businesses to access world-class eco-packaging at competitive prices.

Why Greece?

Greece stands at a pivotal moment in its transition toward sustainable business practices. The hospitality sector — one of the largest in Europe — is actively seeking alternatives to conventional single-use packaging. DivinaECO Pack is perfectly positioned to meet this demand with products that combine performance, aesthetics, and environmental responsibility.

"Our mission is simple: bring the same quality we have delivered across five continents directly to the Greek market, with local support and faster delivery." — DivinaECO Pack Greece

What We Offer

Our Greek portfolio covers the full spectrum of food service and retail packaging needs:

  • BIO paper cups (espresso, take-away, cold drinks) — fully biodegradable
  • Kraft paper bags and wrapping — ideal for bakeries and delis
  • Corrugated boxes for grill, fast food and food delivery
  • Custom printed packaging with 10-day lead time
  • Certified compostable cutlery and lids

Local Stock, Fast Delivery

All core products are held in local stock in Athens, meaning orders placed before 14:00 are dispatched same day for Attica region, and within 48 hours for the rest of Greece. For larger custom orders, our production team in India ensures a 10–15 day turnaround.

We are also proud to offer free samples for businesses that would like to test our products before committing to an order. Reach out via our contact page to request yours.
