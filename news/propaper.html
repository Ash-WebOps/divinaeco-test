DivinaECO at ProPaper Dubai 2024 — Showcasing Sustainable Packaging Solutions

September 2024 · DivinaECO Pack Team

We are proud to have participated in ProPaper Dubai 2024, one of the leading exhibitions for paper, packaging and converting industries in the Middle East. The event provided an excellent platform to showcase our eco-friendly packaging solutions to a global audience.

ProPaper Dubai is a key international exhibition that brings together manufacturers, suppliers and buyers from across the paper and packaging industry. The exhibition served as a valuable opportunity for DivinaECO Pack to connect with distributors, importers and business partners from across the Middle East, Europe and Africa.

Date
September 2024
Location
Dubai World Trade Centre
Stand
C-36

Our Presence at ProPaper Dubai

At ProPaper Dubai 2024, we showcased our complete range of eco-friendly packaging products, including paper cups, food containers, and custom printed solutions. Visitors had the opportunity to explore our materials, understand our manufacturing capabilities, and discuss business opportunities directly with our team.

  • Display of single wall, double wall and ripple paper cups
  • Custom printed packaging solutions for global clients
  • Food-grade paperboard and PE-coated materials
  • Eco-friendly and recyclable packaging innovations
  • Discussion on export capabilities and bulk supply

Our team interacting with visitors and partners at ProPaper Dubai 2024

Strengthening Global Partnerships

Our participation at ProPaper Dubai helped us strengthen our international presence and build valuable connections with clients across multiple regions. The exhibition opened new opportunities for collaboration and long-term partnerships in global markets.

ProPaper Dubai 2024 marked an important milestone in our journey towards expanding DivinaECO Pack’s global footprint.

As we continue to grow, we remain committed to delivering high-quality, sustainable packaging solutions tailored for businesses worldwide.
