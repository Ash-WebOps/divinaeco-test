New BIO Cups Range — Launching Soon

15 February 2026 · DivinaECO Pack Team

We are currently developing our new BIO Cups range — a fully biodegradable and compostable solution designed for the espresso and specialty coffee market, launching soon.

The coffee industry is one of the largest single-use packaging consumers in Greece and globally. With our upcoming BIO Cups, espresso bars, cafes, and food service operators can now offer their customers the same great coffee experience with zero compromise on environmental responsibility.

Designed for True Sustainability

Unlike many products on the market that claim to be "eco" without meaningful certification, our BIO Cups carry the EN 13432 industrial composting certification and the OK Compost Home label — meaning they break down in home composting conditions within 12 weeks, leaving no microplastics behind.

Available Sizes & Formats

The BIO Cups range will initially be available in four sizes to cover the full espresso and take-away coffee spectrum:

  • 50ml — Single espresso, ristretto
  • 80ml — Double espresso, lungo
  • 120ml — Cortado, flat white
  • 180ml — Cappuccino, latte macchiato

All sizes are available with matching biodegradable lids and are compatible with standard cup holders and thermal sleeves. Custom printing (minimum 5,000 units) is available with a 15-day lead time.

Our BIO Cups are currently in development and will be available soon. For early access, sampling, or partnership enquiries, feel free to contact our team.
