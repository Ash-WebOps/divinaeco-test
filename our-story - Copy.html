About DivinaECO Pack

Who We Are

At DivinaECO Pack, we are not just suppliers of packaging — we are partners in building sustainable, high-quality solutions for global businesses.

Backed by over three decades of uninterrupted manufacturing heritage, we exist at the intersection of environmental responsibility and outstanding product quality. Every cup, every lid, every sleeve we produce is a statement of intent: that commercial success and ecological care are not opposites.

We serve cafes, restaurants, quick-service chains, hotels, food delivery brands, and FMCG businesses across Europe, the Middle East, and beyond — delivering both stock products for immediate dispatch and fully customised branded packaging, with precision and care.

How It Started

Our Journey

It began with a single production line, a manufacturing floor, and an unshakeable belief that packaging could be done better — more responsibly, more consistently, and at a quality the world deserved.

Our roots go back over 30 years. What started as a focused manufacturing operation producing paper-based packaging has grown, over three decades of persistent work, into a full-scale production facility serving clients across continents. The journey was never linear — it was built through investment, adaptation, and a refusal to cut corners.

The first major shift came with scale. As capacity grew and new machinery came online, export markets opened — Europe first, then further afield. Each new market brought new standards, new expectations, and new reasons to push quality further. We met every one of them.

The second shift was philosophical. As environmental awareness grew worldwide, we made a pivotal decision: to transition entirely to sustainable, food-safe, eco-friendly materials. Plant-based PLA linings. FSC-certified paperboard. 100% recyclable ranges. We didn't follow a trend — we committed to a direction.

Today, DivinaECO Pack operates at the intersection of 30+ years of manufacturing experience and a forward-looking commitment to sustainability. From Athens to the world — we bring the same discipline, the same standards, and the same pride to every order we fulfil.

What We Do Today

Our Products & Services

We are a full-service eco-packaging company — manufacturing, customising, and exporting sustainable paper-based packaging to businesses worldwide.

Our core product range is built around paper cups — from single wall and double wall to ripple wall — designed for hot and cold beverage service, built to food-safe standards, and available in stock sizes or fully branded custom print. We also produce a range of packaging solutions including grill boxes, food containers, and sleeve packaging for the HORECA and food service markets.

Custom printing is a core part of what we offer. Our in-house print capability means brands can put their identity on every cup and box — with precise colour reproduction, consistent results at scale, and no reliance on third parties.

Our export markets include the United States, the United Kingdom, the Middle East, and across Europe. Every market we serve receives the same standard of quality, the same responsiveness, and the same commitment to getting it right.

How We Make It

In-House Manufacturing Excellence

Every product bearing the DivinaECO name is conceived, designed, and produced entirely within our own facility — giving us complete control over quality, consistency, and customisation at every step.

Our Production Floor State-of-the-Art Facility

Unlike distributors who resell products, DivinaECO operates its own end-to-end production line. From raw paperboard sourcing to cup forming, printing and final QC — everything happens under one roof, under our direct supervision.

This vertically integrated model means shorter lead times, tighter tolerances, superior consistency, and the flexibility to accommodate custom orders that third-party distributors simply cannot match.

  • Single, Double & Triple Wall cup forming lines — catering to hot beverages, cold drinks and specialty coffee.
  • Offset & Flexo Printing up to 4 colours — precise logo reproduction on every unit.
  • Plant-Based PLA Lining — food-safe, biodegradable inner coating replacing conventional plastic films.
  • Grill Boxes & Food Packaging — robust, grease-resistant kraft-based packaging for HORECA and food service.
  • In-House Quality Control — every batch is tested for dimensional accuracy, leak integrity and print fidelity before dispatch.
  • Custom MOQ from 10,000 pcs — accessible custom print runs for growing brands without inflated minimums.
